BODMAS

Understanding BODMAS Analysis and the Calculator Algorithm

What is BODMAS?

BODMAS is the mathematical rule used to determine the correct order for evaluating arithmetic expressions. Without BODMAS, the same expression could produce different answers. Every scientific and standard calculator follows this order when solving mathematical expressions.

Letter Meaning
B Brackets
O Orders (Powers & Roots)
D Division
M Multiplication
A Addition
S Subtraction

BODMAS Analysis

When a user enters an expression into a calculator, the calculator does not immediately perform the calculations from left to right. Instead, it first analyzes the expression to identify numbers, operators, and brackets before applying the BODMAS rule.

Example

Expression 12 + 5 × 4 - 18 ÷ 3 Step 1 Multiply 5 × 4 = 20 Expression becomes 12 + 20 - 18 ÷ 3 Step 2 Divide 18 ÷ 3 = 6 Expression becomes 12 + 20 - 6 Step 3 Add 12 + 20 = 32 Step 4 Subtract 32 - 6 = 26 Final Answer = 26

Complete Algorithm (Idea Process)

Below is the logical process used to create a BODMAS calculator function.

START ↓ Read User Input ↓ Remove Spaces ↓ Validate Expression • Empty Input • Invalid Symbols • Missing Brackets • Invalid Decimal Numbers • Consecutive Operators ↓ Tokenize Expression ↓ Solve Brackets ↓ Solve Orders ↓ Solve Multiplication ↓ Solve Division ↓ Solve Addition ↓ Solve Subtraction ↓ Display Final Answer ↓ END
